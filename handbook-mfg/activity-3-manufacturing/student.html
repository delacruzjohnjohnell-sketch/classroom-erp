A real small factory run by a team over two months: February and March 2027. Each of you holds one or more roles, and every step below belongs to a role.

Duration 2 months · Company a TEAM company made by your teacher (Standard costing) · Steps 557 in all, spread over the roles · Currency / tax Philippine peso, 12% VAT · Checked by your teacher on the company itself; nothing to hand in.

1. The business

Capiz Lantern Works makes lamps from capiz shell: three semi-finished parts (small shade, large shade, brass base) become five lamps (small and large pendant, table lamp, floor lamp, four-arm chandelier). Ten materials are bought from five vendors, shells by the bundle (5 kg) but stocked by the kg. Six customers order lamps. The owner puts in ₱1,500,000 and buys a capiz cutting machine.

2. Your team and your roles

Your teacher decides how many of you there are and who holds which role, and can change it at any time (Teams screen). One person can hold several roles; several people can hold the same role. Do only the steps of the roles you hold. The system refuses anything else (for example, a warehouse clerk cannot create a purchase order) and tells you which role you would need.

3. Read this before you start

  1. Work in step order. The # column is the order of the whole company. If the step just before yours belongs to another role and is not done yet, wait or ask that teammate.
  2. Type the date shown on every form. Most forms start with today's date; the dates drive the books, the closing and the check. Issue material posts at once: set its Date first.
  3. Names and SKUs must match exactly. The check finds items by SKU and vendors and customers by name.
  4. QUARANTINE. Items that are inspected on receipt, and every finished lamp, go to the QUARANTINE store first. Nothing there can be used or sold until Quality has decided.
  5. Two people for approvals. A voucher that needs approval (override ticked) and a vendor invoice on hold are approved or released by the Finance manager, never by the person who prepared them.
  6. Months close in order. Settle every order, then the month, then close it. February must be closed before March is closed.
  7. Do not use Payroll, Banking or the maintenance log: they are not part of this activity. Payroll is a plain journal voucher here.
  8. Do not delete or edit posted documents. If a step says reverse, use Reverse; if you made a mistake, tell your teacher.
TIP The dates are in the future compared with today on purpose. The screens allow it, and the month-end check will say a month is not over yet: that is fine for this activity.
